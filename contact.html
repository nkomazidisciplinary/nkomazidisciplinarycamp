Frequently Asked Contact Questions

How can I contact the camp organisers?

You can call or WhatsApp us on 076 329 2437.

Where are your offices located?

We have two offices: Tonga Glory City Mall, and 2nd Blessings Supermarket, D797, Nkomazi, Mpumalanga. Choose the one nearest to you.

Can I enquire about registration through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can contact the organisers on WhatsApp for registration information and assistance.

Yes. Our office is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00, and closed on Saturday and Sunday. Please contact the organisers to arrange a visit.
