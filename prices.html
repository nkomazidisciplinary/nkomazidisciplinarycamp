Registration Fee

R500

NON-REFUNDABLE

Paying the R500 registration fee registers you and reserves a space. It is part of the camp package price, not an extra charge, and it is non-refundable, whether or not the camp has started. After paying it, the remaining camp price is settled. If you cancel before the programme begins, the full amount you paid is refunded, excluding the R500. Once the programme has begun, a refund is still given, but not the full amount, and the R500 is excluded. Please contact the organisers to confirm payment arrangements.

Understanding Your Camp Package

How to Register

1. Choose Your Package

Explore the available camp durations and select your preferred package.

2. Complete Registration

Complete the online application form with the applicant’s details, including ID/Passport number and home address, your preferred package, and the parent or guardian’s details if under 18.

3. Contact the Organisers

Pay into one of the bank accounts on the Register Now page, then confirm your payment with the management.

Before You Register

  • Confirm the available camp dates.
  • Select the appropriate package duration.
  • Confirm the R500 registration fee.
  • Review the camp’s registration requirements.
  • Obtain the relevant parent or guardian consent, where the participant is under 18.
  • Confirm payment arrangements, and confirm your payment with the management after paying.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the one-week package cost?

The advertised one-week package costs R1,500.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The R500 registration fee reserves your space, is part of the package price and is non-refundable.

Can I register for three months?

Yes, a three-month package is advertised at R20,000. Availability and dates should be confirmed with the organisers.

How do I register?

Use the online registration form or contact the organisers on 076 329 2437.
