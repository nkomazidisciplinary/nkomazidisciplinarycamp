What to Bring to Camp

Dear Parents/Guardians,

To ensure your child has a comfortable and fulfilling stay at the camp, please ensure they bring the following:

Clothing

(Not expensive clothes)

  • T-shirts and long-sleeve shirts (sufficient for the duration of their stay)
  • Shorts and trousers/jeans
  • Underwear and socks (enough for daily changes)
  • Pyjamas/sleepwear
  • Warm jacket or sweater
  • Comfortable walking shoes or sneakers
  • Sandals or flip-flops
  • Hat or cap (for sun protection)
  • Raincoat or poncho
  • Disciplinary camp T-shirt R200 (Compulsory)

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Soap and washing powder
  • Toilet paper
  • Comb or hairbrush
  • Towel (bath and face towel)
  • Round/oval bathtub basin

Bedding

  • Sponge and blanket
  • Pillow with pillowcase

Personal Items

  • Bible (mandatory)
  • Notebook and pens/pencils
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Torch/flashlight with spare batteries
  • Personal medication (if applicable, with instructions)

Recreational and Outdoor Items

  • Sportswear for physical activities
  • Conti suit/worksuit/overalls (khovas)
  • Gumboots
  • Small backpack for day activities

Prohibited Items

  • Electric devices (e.g., gaming consoles, tablets, cellphones or laptops)
  • Expensive jewellery or valuables
  • Sharp objects or weapons of any kind
  • Non-prescription drugs or alcohol

Note: The camp will provide camping tents and meals. Please ensure all personal belongings are clearly labelled with your child’s name.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact us at 076 329 2437.

Kind regards,
Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp Management.
