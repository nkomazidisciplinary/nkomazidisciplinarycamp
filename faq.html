Frequently Asked Questions

General Camp Information

What is Mpumalanga Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp?

A camp initiative focused on promoting discipline, teamwork, responsibility and personal development through organised camp experiences and structured activities.

What is the purpose of the camp?

To encourage positive behaviour, self-discipline, respect, teamwork and responsibility through structured activities and shared experiences.

Where is the camp located?

Our offices are at Tonga Glory City Mall and Mzinti, 2nd Blessings Supermarket, D797, Nkomazi, Mpumalanga. Please contact the organisers to confirm the exact camp venue and directions.

When will the camp take place?

The advertised camp period is November and December 2026. Contact the organisers to confirm starting dates and programme schedules.

Who can attend the camp?

The camp is for participants interested in discipline, personal development and structured camp activities. Age requirements will be confirmed by the organisers.

Programmes and Activities

What activities will participants take part in?

Activities include physical training and sporting activities, outdoor recreational learning, addiction recovery therapy and counselling, farming and agricultural activities, community service excursions, and life skills training and other camp-related programmes.

Does the camp include physical training?

Yes. Physical training and sporting activities are part of the camp. Please contact the organisers to confirm the type, intensity and frequency of the activities.

What does Teamwork and Unity involve?

Participants work together, communicate, respect one another and develop cooperation through group activities.

What does Discipline and Security mean?

This is one of the advertised focus areas. Contact the organisers to clarify whether it covers general discipline and supervision, security-related training or both.

Will participants receive a certificate?

Certificate availability and requirements will be confirmed by the camp organisers.

Registration and Requirements

How do I register for the camp?

Contact the organisers on WhatsApp or phone, 076 329 2437, or complete the online application form on the Register Now page.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The registration fee is R500. Paying it registers you and reserves a space. It is part of the package price, not an extra charge, and it is non-refundable.

What information is required when registering?

The application form asks for the applicant’s full name, ID/Passport number, contact number, home address, city or town and province; your preferred package; medical and emergency information; and the parent or guardian’s details if the applicant is under 18. You also accept the camp’s terms and conditions and complete the consent and indemnity declaration. The organisers will communicate any additional requirements.

Can a parent or guardian register a participant?

Yes. A parent or legal guardian should assist with registration where the participant is a minor and give the necessary consent.

Can I register more than one participant?

Please contact the organisers to confirm the process and any special arrangements.

Packages and Payments

What camp packages are available?

1 Week R1,500, 2 Weeks R2,500, 3 Weeks R3,500, 1 Month R5,000, 2 Months R10,000 and 3 Months R20,000. See the Camp Prices page.

Is the R500 registration fee included in the package price?

Yes. The R500 registration fee is part of the package price, not an extra charge. After paying it, you settle the remaining camp price. The R500 is non-refundable.

What is included in the package price?

Fees cover 3 meals per day, 24-hour security, camping tents, sports gear, training, camp activities and supervision. A camp T-shirt (R200) is compulsory.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payments can be made into either of the bank accounts (FNB or Capitec) shown on the Register Now page. After paying, please confirm your payment with the management on 076 329 2437. Contact the organisers to confirm payment deadlines and whether instalments are available.

Safety and Parent/Guardian Information

How are participants supervised during the camp?

The organisers will provide information about supervision, responsible staff, emergency procedures and participant safety before registration is completed.

What should participants bring to the camp?

A full packing list is on the Register Now page. It covers clothing (including the compulsory R200 camp T-shirt), toiletries, bedding, personal items (a Bible is mandatory) and recreational and outdoor items. Electronic devices such as cellphones and tablets, expensive jewellery, sharp objects and non-prescription drugs or alcohol are prohibited. The camp provides camping tents and meals.

What happens if a participant has a medical condition or special requirement?

Medical conditions and allergies are recorded on the application form, which also includes consent for first aid and medical treatment in an emergency. Please also tell the organisers before the camp begins so appropriate arrangements can be discussed.

Can parents or guardians contact participants during the camp?

Please contact the organisers to confirm communication arrangements, visiting rules and the contact person.

What happens if a participant can no longer attend?

The R500 registration fee is non-refundable, whether or not the camp has started. If you cancel before the programme begins, the full amount you paid is refunded, excluding the R500. Once the programme has begun, a refund is given but not the full amount, and the R500 is excluded. Please read the camp’s terms and conditions on the Register Now page before paying.
